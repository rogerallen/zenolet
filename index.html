About Zenolet

Zenolet is an elegant, distraction-free web reader for exploring and reading timeless literary classics from Project Gutenberg.

Curated Offline-first Bookshelf

Focus on just eight books. Each book stays pinned to its slot with saved reading progress, cover art, and local storage size. Adding a book caches its full EPUB archive and illustrations locally in your browser. Read anywhere off-grid with zero network requests.

Tactile Horizontal Reading Engine

Paginated book spreads with single or double column layouts, responsive font scaling, and reading themes.

Seamless Mobile Handoff

Scan the on-screen QR code with your phone camera to transfer your book and exact reading position instantly without accounts or logins.

Forkable & Sovereign Micro-Library

Zenolet is open-source and easily forked on GitHub. Deploy your own static library with custom curation, branding, and a free Cloudflare CORS proxy.
